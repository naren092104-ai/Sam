Our Story

Four generations. One jar.

  1. 1
    1978

    Grandmother's First Jar

    It began on a Hyderabad terrace — Ammamma's avakaya recipe, written in the margin of a prayer book.

  2. 2
    1996

    A Family Heritage

    Her daughters carried the recipe across kitchens, refining each spice ratio with seasons.

  3. 3
    2018

    Modern Glass, Same Soul

    We swapped clay urns for food-grade glass — never the recipe, never the hand-mixing.

  4. 4
    Today

    Pan India, Door to Door

    10,000+ kitchens, 800+ pincodes. Same family. Same 21-day cure. Same flavour.
